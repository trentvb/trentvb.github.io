Approach & AI Delivery

Document Conversion & File-Based RAG

In industrial and enterprise engineering, documents are dense: electrical specifications, schedules, multi-column blueprints, and tables. Naive text splitting destroys this context. My work on the AI Delivery Team centers on transforming messy unstructured files into high-fidelity, structured representations:

01
Layout-Aware Ingestion: Extracting text from PDFs, Office files, and technical specs while preserving H1–H4 section hierarchy, list numbering, and structural boundaries.
02
Table & Structural Fidelity: Translating complex tabular data into clean Markdown tables so language models can parse rows, columns, and metric labels without spatial confusion.
03
File-Based Knowledge Repositories (OKF): Organizing documents into transparent, version-controlled Markdown files with standardized YAML frontmatter metadata, keeping the knowledge layer deterministic, auditable, and lightweight.
04
Selective Context Packaging: Navigating document hierarchy to extract precise, coherent sections with parent breadcrumbs rather than sending noisy token fragments to model prompts.
05
Grounding & Local Benchmarks: Anchoring model responses directly to source document citations, while benchmarking prompt performance against local models (Ollama, llama.cpp) on an on-premise home server.
